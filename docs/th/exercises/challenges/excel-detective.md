## Get ready

- Open your morning `Asteria_Service_KPI_Reviewed.xlsx`, or download the [reviewed backup](/files/Asteria_Service_KPI_Reviewed_backup.xlsx). Check its values before using it.
- Use synthetic Asteria data only. Save a group copy; do not overwrite source files.
- Rotate the Operator and Evidence checker. In a group of three, combine Challenger and Presenter. See the [role guide](../05-group-challenge#prepare-your-group-and-files).

## Practice 1: Level 1 — Find insights with evidence

**Primary target:** Select one priority from three verified insights.

1. Open the chosen workbook in **Excel**. Start with your morning analysis or ask Copilot:

   ```text
   Using the Asteria data in this workbook, suggest three insights a manager should consider. State the period, values and source table or columns for each. Do not infer cause and effect without supporting evidence. Write in English.
   ```

2. The Evidence checker verifies at least one source value per insight. Correct or replace any inaccurate insight.
3. The Challenger checks whether the evidence supports the conclusions. Choose one issue to address first and give one reason.
4. Record the three insights and one priority in a new sheet or short document you can display. No extra slides are required.

### Quick check

- All three insights have verifiable values or evidence.
- The priority is a reasoned recommendation, not an approved decision.

**Level 1 output:** Three verified insights and one priority.

Passing these checks completes the required challenge. Start Level 2 only if time remains before the instructor’s checking/rehearsal cue.

## Practice 2: Level 2 — Stress-test your priority

**Primary target:** Test whether your chosen priority still holds when compared with another date or period.

1. Select one metric supporting your priority. Choose another date or a comparable period from the same workbook. Use the same metric and units; avoid comparing totals from periods of unequal length.
2. Give Copilot only the synthetic values and dates you verified, together with your current priority. Ask:

   ```text
   Stress-test our selected priority using the two Asteria dates or periods and verified values supplied here. Explain whether the comparison strengthens or weakens our priority. State one limitation of this evidence. Use the same metric and units. Do not infer causes or invent missing values. Write in English.
   ```

3. Check both values against the workbook. Decide whether to keep or revise the priority; a comparison that weakens it is a useful finding.
4. Add a short comparison, your decision and one evidence limitation beside your Level 1 findings in the same sheet or document.

**Level 2 output:** Your original findings plus one verified comparison, a keep/revise decision and one limitation.

### Quick check

- Both comparison values, dates or periods, and units match the source.
- The priority decision reflects the comparison and acknowledges one evidence limitation.
