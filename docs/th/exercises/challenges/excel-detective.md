## Choose your approach

Start in the named app, then use **at least two of Word, Excel and PowerPoint meaningfully**. Use Copilot at least once. You choose the app order, prompts and division of work; you can work in parallel or revisit an app.

The second document app must contribute useful analysis, drafting, visualization or review. Merely opening a file does not count. One final deliverable is enough if you can show the supporting work. Manual editing and copying checked content between apps are valid; automatic transfer is not required.

- Choose the service question, audience and way to explain your findings.
- Word could help develop a concise brief; PowerPoint could help tell a visual story. You decide what each app contributes.

## Level 1 — Complete your mission

<PracticeIllustration id="excel-detective-level-1" alt="A group selects a priority from three checked findings." />

**Primary target:** Three verified insights and one reasoned priority, communicated through Word or PowerPoint with meaningful Excel work behind them.

**Level 1 output:** Three verified insights and one reasoned priority, communicated through Word or PowerPoint with meaningful Excel work behind them.

### Quick check

- Each insight has a checked source value, date or period, and unit; the priority is supported without claiming an unproven cause.
- Your group can show a meaningful contribution from at least two document apps and one Copilot contribution that you checked or corrected.

## Level 2 — Strengthen your result

<PracticeIllustration id="excel-detective-level-2" alt="Colleagues compare two periods to test their chosen priority." />

**Primary target:** Improve the existing result through a focused evidence or audience test.

Test your priority against another date or comparable period. You choose the comparison and how to communicate whether it strengthens or weakens the case. Keep the same metric and units; avoid comparing totals for unequal-length periods.

**Level 2 output:** Your revised findings with one checked comparison, a keep-or-revise decision and one evidence limitation.

**Optional Copilot question** — supply the relevant synthetic evidence and feedback first, and adapt this question as you wish.

```text
Review this comparison and our conclusion. Does the evidence justify keeping or changing the priority? Identify one limitation without inventing missing facts. Write in English.
```

### Quick check

- The comparison uses verified values and comparable periods and units.
- The priority decision follows the evidence and names one limitation.

</details>

## Ready to share

Save your group copy and open the result and supporting work you will show. Explain why you chose your apps and point out one verified or corrected Copilot contribution. No additional showcase deck is required.

Stop Level 2 at the instructor’s checking/rehearsal cue. **Level 1 alone is a successful result.** Show completed and unfinished work honestly within the existing showcase turn.
