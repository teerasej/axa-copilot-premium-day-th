**Start in Word.** A colleague missed the service recovery meeting. Build a handover that makes the confirmed work, dependencies and next decision easy to understand and follow.

## Get ready

[Download starter pack](/files/challenges/meeting-to-momentum.zip)

Extract the ZIP and read `START-HERE.md`. Open `Asteria_Teams_Meeting_Recap.docx` in **Word**. You may use your relevant morning Asteria outputs instead of the prepared starting files. Save group copies without overwriting the sources.

**Individual files**

- [Asteria_Teams_Meeting_Recap.docx](/files/Asteria_Teams_Meeting_Recap.docx) — Word meeting recap containing confirmed actions and unresolved dependencies.
- [Asteria_Executive_Review_Transcript.vtt](/files/Asteria_Executive_Review_Transcript.vtt) — Authoritative transcript for optional direct reading. Do not attach VTT to Copilot.

The recap records later confirmed decisions. Earlier proposals in the morning evidence brief are not a replacement for this meeting record. Dates in the source belong to the fictional case, not today’s workshop.

Use synthetic Asteria material only. Rotate the Operator and Evidence checker; in a group of three combine Challenger and Presenter. See the [role guide](../05-group-challenge#prepare-your-group-and-files).

## Choose your approach

Start in the named app, then use **at least two of Word, Excel and PowerPoint meaningfully**. Use Copilot at least once. You choose the app order, prompts and division of work; you can work in parallel or revisit an app.

The second document app must contribute useful analysis, drafting, visualization or review. Merely opening a file does not count. One final deliverable is enough if you can show the supporting work. Manual editing and copying checked content between apps are valid; automatic transfer is not required.

- Choose the handover format and the information that deserves emphasis.
- Excel could make actions easier to track; PowerPoint could make status and dependencies easier to discuss. Choose your own route from the Word recap.



## Level 1 — Complete your mission

**Primary target:** A handover covering at least three confirmed actions, their owners, dates and dependencies, with useful supporting work in Excel or PowerPoint.

**Level 1 output:** A handover covering at least three confirmed actions, their owners, dates and dependencies, with useful supporting work in Excel or PowerPoint.

### Quick check

- Actions and commitments match the recap; missing dates and unresolved access remain explicit rather than invented or marked complete.
- Your group can show a meaningful contribution from at least two document apps and one Copilot contribution that you checked or corrected.

## Level 2 — Strengthen your result

**Primary target:** Improve the existing result through a focused evidence or audience test.

Ask a colleague to identify the blocked action and the next decision from your handover. Improve the result wherever their reading exposes ambiguity.

**Level 2 output:** The same handover refined so the blocked action and next decision are clear.

**Optional Copilot question** — supply the relevant synthetic evidence and feedback first, and adapt this question as you wish.

```text
Review whether our handover makes the blocked action and next decision clear. Use this colleague’s feedback and preserve the meeting record. Write in English.
```

### Quick check

- The unresolved Security-access dependency and the next decision gate are visible.
- The revised handover preserves confirmed owners and dates and labels missing information.

</details>

## Ready to share

Save your group copy and open the result and supporting work you will show. Explain why you chose your apps and point out one verified or corrected Copilot contribution. No additional showcase deck is required.

Stop Level 2 at the instructor’s checking/rehearsal cue. **Level 1 alone is a successful result.** Show completed and unfinished work honestly within the existing showcase turn.
