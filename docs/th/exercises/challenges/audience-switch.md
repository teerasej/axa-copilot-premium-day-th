# Audience Switch

<ExerciseHeaderImage src="/images/exercise-headers/03-communicate-accountably.png" alt="Colleagues adapting a message for different readers" />

**Start in Word.** Leadership and the delivery team need different explanations of the same evidence. Choose one verified finding and make it useful to both audiences without changing its meaning.

## Get ready

[Download starter pack](/files/challenges/audience-switch.zip)

Extract the ZIP and read `START-HERE.md`. Open `Asteria_Grounded_Evidence_Brief.docx` in **Word**. You may use your relevant morning Asteria outputs instead of the prepared starting files. Save group copies without overwriting the sources.

**Individual files**

- [Asteria_Grounded_Evidence_Brief.docx](/files/Asteria_Grounded_Evidence_Brief.docx) — Prepared checked reference describing the pre-meeting position. Preserve its uncertainty and source locations.
- [Asteria_Service_KPI_28days.xlsx](/files/Asteria_Service_KPI_28days.xlsx) — Original 28-day values, dates, formulas and context notes for analysis and checking.
- [Asteria_Operational_Context.docx](/files/Asteria_Operational_Context.docx) — Business questions and evidence boundaries; recorded events do not prove causes.

The evidence brief describes the **pre-meeting position**. The later meeting recap contains subsequently confirmed decisions; do not silently mix these timeframes. For this mission, use the supplied pre-meeting reference.

Use synthetic Asteria material only. Rotate the Operator and Evidence checker; in a group of three combine Challenger and Presenter. See the [role guide](../05-group-challenge#prepare-your-group-and-files).

## Choose your approach

Start in the named app, then use **at least two of Word, Excel and PowerPoint meaningfully**. Use Copilot at least once. You choose the app order, prompts and division of work; you can work in parallel or revisit an app.

The second document app must contribute useful analysis, drafting, visualization or review. Merely opening a file does not count. One final deliverable is enough if you can show the supporting work. Manual editing and copying checked content between apps are valid; automatic transfer is not required.

- Leadership needs the significance, decision or uncertainty. The delivery team needs practical implications, evidence and what to check next.
- Choose the tone and formats. PowerPoint could tell a visual story; Excel could check or visualize the evidence. A second app must create or substantively support at least one version.



## Level 1 — Complete your mission

**Primary target:** Two audience-specific explanations of one verified finding, with meaningful Word work and a contribution from Excel or PowerPoint.

**Level 1 output:** Two audience-specific explanations of one verified finding, with meaningful Word work and a contribution from Excel or PowerPoint.

### Quick check

- The two versions meet different reader needs while preserving the same values, period, uncertainty and proposal status.
- Your group can show a meaningful contribution from at least two document apps and one Copilot contribution that you checked or corrected.

## Level 2 — Strengthen your result

**Primary target:** Improve the existing result through a focused evidence or audience test.

Test both versions with peers representing the two audiences. Refine the emphasis, wording or visuals where the intended message is misunderstood.

**Level 2 output:** The same two versions improved using audience feedback.

**Optional Copilot question** — supply the relevant synthetic evidence and feedback first, and adapt this question as you wish.

```text
Compare these two audience versions with the feedback we received. Suggest improvements in emphasis or wording while keeping the same evidence and uncertainty. Write in English.
```

### Quick check

- Each revision responds to a specific reader need or misunderstanding.
- The core finding and its evidence boundaries remain consistent across versions.

</details>

## Ready to share

Save your group copy and open the result and supporting work you will show. Explain why you chose your apps and point out one verified or corrected Copilot contribution. No additional showcase deck is required.

Stop Level 2 at the instructor’s checking/rehearsal cue. **Level 1 alone is a successful result.** Show completed and unfinished work honestly within the existing showcase turn.
