# Start here — Asteria Copilot Workshop

All files describe fictional training material, not real AXA operational data.

## Core course files

The eight source files are in this folder. The four prepared backups and examples are in `Prepared assets`. Keep their names and save your work as separate copies.

## Choose a group challenge

Open `Group challenges`, then the folder named after your chosen challenge. Each folder includes all its inputs and its own `START-HERE.md`, which identifies the starting file and links to the full online mission. Shared files are intentionally copied into each folder so you do not need to search elsewhere. You only complete one challenge.

Choose your mission: https://teerasej.github.io/axa-copilot-premium-day-th/th/exercises/05-group-challenge

Already downloaded this full course ZIP? You do not need another challenge download. Relevant morning Asteria outputs are also valid inputs.

## Begin safely

Extract this ZIP once before opening the files. Follow the online preparation instructions to upload the extracted course folder to your practice OneDrive. Do not upload ZIP files to Copilot. If folder upload is unavailable, prepare the core files first and upload only your chosen challenge folder's Office files when needed. Read Markdown guides and VTT transcripts directly; do not attach them to Copilot.

Preparation: https://teerasej.github.io/axa-copilot-premium-day-th/th/before-you-begin#prepare-your-files

Use synthetic data only. Keep original inputs intact, save group copies and keep real emails, meetings and private data out of group work.
